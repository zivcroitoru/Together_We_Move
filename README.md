# Prototype 3: Together We Move

## Overview
**Together We Move** is a cooperative puzzle-platformer where two children control separate characters (Blue and Red) in the same shared world[cite: 1]. Players must assist each other to open pathways, activate mechanisms, and reach the exit together[cite: 1].

Designed for autistic children, separate avatars establish clear individual ownership while structured obstacles promote mutual support, spatial awareness, and turn-taking[cite: 1].

---

## Core Mechanics
* **Dual Characters:** Each child controls their own avatar with dedicated movement and interaction capabilities[cite: 1].
* **Asymmetrical Assistance:** Progress relies on one player holding switches or operating levers to create pathways for the other[cite: 1].
* **Shared Completion:** A level is only completed when both characters successfully reach the goal[cite: 1].

---

## Level Breakdown

| Level | Title | Mechanics & Core Objective | Developmental & Therapeutic Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Hold the Platform** | Player 1 stands on a floor button to extend a bridge; Player 2 crosses and pulls a permanent lever so Player 1 can cross[cite: 1]. | Patience, cause-and-effect understanding, helping before advancing[cite: 1]. |
| **2** | **Take Turns Leading** | Red opens a bridge for Blue; Blue crosses to open a gate for Red; Red activates the final path; both meet at the goal[cite: 1]. | Balanced participation, reciprocal aid (giving and receiving help), partner tracking[cite: 1]. |
| **3** | **Stay Together** | Both characters navigate while staying inside a shared glowing proximity circle ("friendship zone")[cite: 1]. Leaving the zone disables bridges and activates hazards[cite: 1]. | Shared attention, coordinated pacing, waiting, and cooperative movement[cite: 1]. |

---

## Key Benefits
* **Intuitive Agency:** Distinct characters provide personal clarity while necessitating active collaboration[cite: 1].
* **Predictable Structure:** Consistent visual cues reduce anxiety and clarify when to act versus when to support[cite: 1].
* **Natural Dialogue Prompts:** Encourages practical communication (e.g., "Are you ready?") before releasing switches[cite: 1].
